# Play Builder

> **Status: v0.1, in testing.** Feedback very welcome.

Turn what your best sellers actually do into plays that anyone on the team can run, without the expert in the room.

## A play is not general advice

"Ask good questions" is best practice. A **play** is best practice tied to a **specific situation**, so a seller knows *when* to use it and *exactly what to do*. If you can't name the trigger, it isn't a play yet.

## What it does

1. **Gathers the raw material:** rough stories, win/loss notes, top-performer interviews or call transcripts. If you're stuck for examples, it interviews you with memory-jogging questions about specific moments: deals won against the odds, rescues, approaches you told others to copy, and mistakes you wished there were a rule for.
2. **Finds the play inside the story:** the trigger, the core move, why it works and the evidence. Then it runs three checks:
   - **Play or strategy?** If it needs a team or budget decision, it's a go-to-market strategy, not a play.
   - **Repeatable?** Can an average seller run it, or does it need a specialist?
   - **Honest?** No deception, pressure or disrespect. If a story contains a tactic like that, the skill keeps the honest principle behind it and offers an honest version.
3. **Writes a one-page play card:** when to use it (and when not to), steps, talk track, discovery questions, objections, proof, common mistakes, how to measure it, and a coaching tip. Short enough to read two minutes before a meeting.

## Try it with the examples

The `examples` folder holds three plays built from real stories, anonymised:

| Play | Built from | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `hidden-use-case-play.md` | A buyer who ruled out a product, then signed a global deal for it a year later | Turning "we don't need that" into a new deal |
| `expert-peer-advisor-play.md` | A quiet, technical senior buyer who invited weekly sessions with his team | Winning trust through expertise, with honest limits on repeatability |
| `helpful-stranger-cold-call-play.md` | A seller who built pipeline by phone where no one else called | The honesty check in action: one tactic was rewritten |

## Who it's for

Sales enablement, GTM programs and sales leadership teams who want to capture what works and spread it, and any seller who wants to turn their own wins into something they can teach.

## Works well with

The skills in this repository tell one story: **coach the call → package the play → run it as a measured program.**

- **Sales Transcript Coach** spots strong behaviour on real calls, which Play Builder can turn into plays, and checks whether sellers run the plays.
- **GTM Program Designer** rolls a play out across teams and markets as a measured program.
