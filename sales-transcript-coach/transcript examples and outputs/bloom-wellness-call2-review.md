**Coaching points**

1. **Situation:** Rachel challenged the €250k quarterly minimum.
   **Behaviour:** James held the line at first, then promised a pilot budget "that works for both sides".
   **Impact:** the floor now sounds negotiable before anyone has approved that internally.
   **Try this:** "Let me confirm what's possible internally, and I'll come back with a pilot structure we can stand behind."
2. **Situation:** Rachel set out three unknowns: vendor, category proof and pricing.
   **Behaviour:** James moved straight on without confirming them.
   **Impact:** the proposal risks being built on his reading of her concerns, not hers.
   **Try this:** "So the proposal needs to cover category proof, cost compared with Meta, and our track record. Is that the full list?"

**What went well**

- Turned the case-study objection into a fair question about methodology, which Rachel accepted.
